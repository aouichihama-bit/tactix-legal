Attendance

Who came today? Ten seconds.

Tap the players who are here. Over the season you see who comes every week and who is drifting away, before they quit.

  • Quick take for today, or a month grid for the whole squad
  • Training groups and game days
  • Statistics for each player, with streaks and monthly bars
سجل الحضور الشهري والسريع مع إحصاءات لكل لاعب
Roster

Your team, and every parent's number.

Every player with up to three phone numbers, so a parent is one tap away. Names can be written in two languages, and positions are the ones you define.

  • Add players one by one, or paste a whole list at once
  • Search, sort and split the squad into groups
  • Print the roster on your club's letterhead

Training

Plan the week. Draw the play.

Build sessions from your own drill library, and draw plays on your sport's real court with arrows, dribbles and screens.

  • Starter drills for every sport to begin with
  • Weekly plans and session sheets you can print
  • Send drills to another coach with a private code
خطط أسبوعية ومكتبة تمارين ولوحات رسم الخطط
Calendar & record

Every match, and how the season is going.

Games and training days on one month view, with a countdown to the next match on your home screen. Enter the scores and Tactix keeps the record.

  • Won, drawn and lost, scored and conceded
  • Form from the last five games
  • Upcoming and past fixtures in one list

Player progress

Coach the steps, not a score.

Technique checklists you can actually coach against, step by step. Log height and weight to see growth over time, and share a report with parents.

  • Checklists for each skill, ticked as the player gets there
  • Growth charts from height and weight logs
  • Reports for parents as a PDF

PDFs

Print it on your club's letterhead.

The roster, a training session, an attendance report or a player's progress becomes a clean PDF with your club's name and colours, ready to print or send.

  • Plays print exactly as you drew them
  • Empty boxes for notes on the day

Fees & tools

Who has paid, and the tools for the court.

Fee collection is there for academies that charge, with receipts and real balances. Clubs that don't can leave it off.

  • Receipts and a balance for each player
  • Chronometer, interval timer and scoreboard
  • Team splitter and announcement maker

Pricing

Free to start.

One team with nine players is free, for as long as you like. Premium is for coaches with more.

Free

One team, nine players

  • Roster, attendance and calendar
  • Training plans and play boards
  • PDF exports on club letterhead
  • Works offline, in all six languages
Premium · yearly

Every team, every player

  • Unlimited teams and players
  • Cloud backup and sync across devices
  • Player evaluations and growth tracking
  • Fee collection with receipts

Built for a sports hall with no signal

Every edit is saved on the phone first and synced when the connection returns. Nothing waits for a network, and nothing is lost when there isn't one.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

This app holds data about children. There is no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and a coach can delete their account and all of its data from inside the app.
