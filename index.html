TACTIX
Everything a coach keeps on paper, in one app.
كل ما يكتبه المدرب على الورق، في تطبيق واحد

Tactix holds a coach's whole season: the roster and parents' numbers, attendance for every session, fixtures, training plans, player progress and fees — and turns any of it into a club-letterhead PDF. It works offline at the court and speaks six languages.

Basketball Football Handball Volleyball Boxing Judo Fencing Taekwondo Karate Athletics Swimming + or name your own sport

What's inside

Roster & contacts

Every player with bilingual names, positions you define yourself, and up to three phone numbers — a parent is one tap from the list. Search, sort, and print on club letterhead.

قوائم اللاعبين بأسماء مزدوجة وأرقام التواصل، مع تصدير PDF

Attendance

A month grid, a one-tap quick take, training groups, game days, and per-player statistics with streaks and monthly bars.

سجل الحضور الشهري والسريع مع إحصاءات لكل لاعب

Calendar & fixtures

Games and training days on one month view, with upcoming and past lists and a countdown to the next match on your home screen.

المباريات وأيام التدريب في تقويم واحد

Training & play boards

Weekly plans, sessions built from a reusable drill library, and plays drawn on your sport's real court — printed exactly as you drew them.

خطط أسبوعية ومكتبة تمارين ولوحات رسم الخطط

Progress & evaluations

Technique checklists you can actually coach against — step by step, not a single score — plus height and weight logs with growth charts, and reports for parents.

تقييم تقني خطوة بخطوة وقياسات النمو وتقارير لأولياء الأمور

Fees & tools

Optional fee collection for academies that charge, with receipts and real balances. Plus a chronometer, interval timer, team splitter, scoreboard and announcement maker.

تحصيل الاشتراكات اختياريًا، وأدوات الملعب: مؤقّت ولوحة نتائج

Built for a sports hall with no signal

Every edit is saved on the phone first and synced when the connection returns. Nothing waits for a network, and nothing is lost when there isn't one.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

This app holds data about children. There is no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and a coach can delete their account and all of its data from inside the app.
