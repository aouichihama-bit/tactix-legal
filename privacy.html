1. Who we are, and who is responsible for what

Tactix is a team-management app for sports coaches, developed by Mohamed Amine Aouichi (Kuwait). It helps coaches manage rosters, attendance, training, evaluations and printable documents. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is protected.

Two different kinds of data pass through Tactix, and our role differs for each:

  • Your coach account — your email address, coach name and subscription status. For this we are the data controller: we decide why and how it is used.
  • The player and team information you enter — for this we are only a data processor. You, the coach (or the club or academy you work for), are the data controller. You decide what to record and why; we simply store it and show it back to you, acting on your instructions. Our obligations in that role are set out in the Data Processing Terms.

2. Data the app stores

  • Account data: your email address and password (managed by Firebase Authentication) — or, if you choose “Continue with Google”, the email address and basic profile (name, picture) of your Google account. Plus your coach name and your licence/subscription status.
  • Team data entered by you (the coach): team names, player names, jersey numbers, positions, dates of birth, residential zone, player and parent phone numbers, player photos, attendance records, physical measurements (height and weight), skill evaluations and free-text notes.
  • App settings: language, theme and similar preferences, plus simple usage counters (for example, how many PDFs you have exported — used only to decide when to show you an upgrade suggestion). All of these are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Tactix contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind — no advertising identifiers, no profiling, and nothing that records how you use the app. We collect nothing beyond what you type into the app and what is needed to sign you in.

There is one exception, and it is worth stating plainly: when the app crashes, it sends us a crash report so the fault can be found and fixed. A report contains the error, the line of code it happened on, and the device model and Android version — never your data. It is deliberately configured to exclude your identity, your IP address, screenshots, and any record of what you were doing beforehand, and any text that looks like a phone number or an email address is removed before the report leaves your phone.

4. Where the data is stored, and international transfers

  • On your device — the app works fully offline and keeps its data locally, stored separately per signed-in account. On the free plan this is the only place your team data lives.
  • In the cloud — with an active Premium subscription and a verified email address, your data is backed up to Google Cloud, linked to your account and protected by access rules so that only you can read it. All transfers use encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections, and data is encrypted at rest by Google.

6. Players, children and your responsibilities as a coach

Tactix is designed for and sold to adult coaches. It is not directed at children, it is not listed in Google Play's Families programme, and children do not have accounts, cannot sign in, and are never contacted by us.

Coaches do, however, record information about players who are usually minors. When you do that, you or your club are the data controller and you are responsible for the lawfulness of that recording. In particular:

  • You must have a valid legal basis for recording each player's data — in practice, the verifiable consent of a parent or legal guardian, or the documented legitimate interests of your club.
  • In the EU, the age at which a child can consent for themselves is set nationally between 13 and 16; below that age, parental consent is required. In the United States, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13.
  • You must tell parents and players what you are recording and why, and let them see, correct or withdraw it.
  • You should only record what you actually need. The app does not require photos, phone numbers, home zone or notes — leave them empty where they are not necessary.

We support you in this: every field is optional except the player's name, all data can be edited or deleted from inside the app at any time, and a full export is available at any moment.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a coach has recorded your child's information without a proper basis, contact the coach or club first, since they control that data. You may also write to us at support@tactix-app.com and we will help identify the account holder and, where appropriate, act to have the data removed.

7. How long we keep data

  • Team and player data — kept for as long as your account exists, because it is your working record. You can delete any part of it at any time; deletions reach the cloud copy on the next sync.
  • Account data — kept while your account is active.
  • After account deletion — your cloud data and sign-in record are erased immediately, and any residual copies in system snapshots are purged within 30 days.
  • Email delivery logs — held by our email provider for a short operational period and then deleted; they contain only the address and the delivery result.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland

You have the right to access your data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Most of these you can exercise yourself, immediately, inside the app: Settings → Save backup gives you a complete machine-readable export, and Settings → Delete account erases everything.

For anything else, write to support@tactix-app.com. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

If you are in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to obtain a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. The same in-app export and deletion tools satisfy these requests immediately.

We want to be unambiguous about the point these laws care about most: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We never have. Tactix contains no advertising and no tracking, so there is nothing to opt out of.

9. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS; stored data is encrypted at rest.
  • Database rules enforce that a signed-in coach can read and write only their own data — this is enforced on the server, not merely in the app.
  • Passwords are never stored by us in readable form; authentication is handled by Google Firebase.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the developer and protected by two-factor authentication.

If a personal data breach ever occurs, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where the law requires, and we will inform affected users and controllers without undue delay.

10. Payments

Tactix Premium is a yearly subscription purchased through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google — we never see or store your card or payment details; we receive only whether a subscription is active.
