These terms apply automatically whenever a coach, club or academy uses Tactix to record information about players. They form the data processing agreement required by Article 28 of the UK and EU GDPR. No signature is needed — but if your club requires a countersigned copy, write to support@tactix-app.com and we will provide one.

1. Roles

You — the coach, club or academy using the app — are the controller of the player and team data you enter. We — Mohamed Amine Aouichi, trading as Tactix — are the processor of that data. For your own coach account data we are a controller in our own right; that is covered by the Privacy Policy instead.

2. What we process, and for how long

  • Subject matter and purpose: storing, displaying, syncing and exporting the team records you create, solely to provide the Tactix service to you.
  • Duration: for as long as your account exists, and no longer.
  • Categories of data subject: players (usually minors), their parents or guardians, and coaching staff you record.
  • Categories of personal data: names, jersey numbers, positions, dates of birth, residential zone, player and parent telephone numbers, photographs, attendance records, height and weight measurements, skill evaluations and free-text notes.
  • Special category data: Tactix is not designed to hold health data. Height and weight are recorded for growth tracking. Please do not enter medical information, injuries or diagnoses in the notes fields — if you do, you are responsible for the additional conditions that Article 9 GDPR imposes.

3. Our obligations

  • We process the data only on your documented instructions. Your use of the app's features is that instruction. We will not use your player data for our own purposes, and never to train machine-learning models.
  • Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality. In practice access is limited to the developer.
  • We apply the technical and organisational measures described in section 9 of the Privacy Policy, as required by Article 32.
  • We assist you in responding to requests from players or parents, and with your obligations on security, breach notification and data protection impact assessments.
  • On deletion of your account we erase all the data; residual system copies are purged within 30 days. You can export a complete copy at any time beforehand from Settings → Save backup.
  • We make available the information needed to demonstrate compliance and will cooperate with audits. Given the size of this service, this normally means answering your questions in writing.

4. Sub-processors

You give general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in section 4 of the Privacy Policy — Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Play Billing) and Resend. We will announce any new or replacement sub-processor on this page at least 30 days in advance, and you may object; if we cannot resolve your objection you may terminate and delete your account.

5. International transfers

Your data is stored in Doha, Qatar, and account emails are triggered from the United States. Neither Kuwait nor Qatar has a European Commission adequacy decision, so these transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum where relevant), which are incorporated into these terms by reference. A copy is available on request.

6. Personal data breaches

We will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting your data, with the information you need to meet your own 72-hour notification deadline.

7. Changes

We may update these terms to reflect changes in the service or the law. Material changes will be announced here with a new effective date and, where they affect your rights, in the app.
