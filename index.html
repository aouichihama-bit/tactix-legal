Attendance

Roll call in ten seconds.

Tap who is here and you are done. Over the weeks Tactix shows you who comes every time and who is drifting away, while there is still time to talk to them.

  • Mark all present, then tap the two who aren't
  • Team and player percentages, a ranking and a month grid
  • Message absent families straight from the list
  • Training groups and match days kept apart
Training & plays

Plan the session. Draw the play.

Build each session from drills with their own timings, then draw them on the pitch or court of your sport. On the day, press Start and Tactix runs the clock drill by drill.

  • Arrows, runs, dribbles, passes, screens and shots
  • Moving plays that animate step by step
  • Starter drills for every sport, and your own drill library
  • Send drills to another coach with a short code
Players

Your squad, and every parent, one tap away.

Each player has a profile with up to three phone numbers, attendance, skills, growth and fitness in one place. Adding a team takes a minute: paste the whole list at once.

  • Call or WhatsApp a parent from the profile
  • Positions you define, groups, search and sorting
  • Names in two languages, for bilingual clubs
Calendar & results

Every match, and how the season is going.

Training days and fixtures on one month view, with a countdown to the next game on your home screen. Enter the score after the final whistle and Tactix keeps the record.

  • Won, drawn and lost, scored and conceded
  • Form from the last five games
  • A tournament planner for leagues and knockouts
Player progress

Coach the technique, not just the score.

Evaluate each skill against a checklist of the steps that make it, so you know exactly what to work on next. Then watch it change over the season.

  • Ready-made checklists for 7 sports, or write your own
  • Fitness tests such as a 30 m sprint, with results over time
  • Height and weight with growth charts
  • A clean report to share with parents

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Write to support@tactix-app.com.

Is Tactix really free?

Yes. One team with up to 30 players is free with no time limit and no ads: attendance, calendar, results, play boards, three training sessions, PDF exports, cloud backup and all the tools. Premium adds more teams, your club letterhead, progress over time, fees and unlimited sessions.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Tactix saves everything on your phone first, so it works in a hall or on a pitch with no signal. When you are back online it syncs to your cloud backup on its own.

Which phones does it run on?

Tactix is an Android app, available on Google Play.

Do parents or players need the app?

No. Tactix is for the coach. Parents get what you send them: a message, a match poster, a progress report or a receipt.

I coach more than one team. Can I use one account?

Yes. With Premium you can run as many teams as you coach, each with its own squad, schedule and records, and switch between them in one tap.

Is my players' data safe?

Your data stays on your phone and in your own private backup. Tactix has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold. You can save a backup file or delete your account at any time. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Premium?

In the Google Play app: Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Tactix. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, and your data stays.
